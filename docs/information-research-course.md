# Information-bearing acquisition: six bounded deliveries

The owner authorized six further research releases on 2026-09-15. This is a
research course, not six promised positive findings. ADRs 0004--0006 apply.
Stock llama.cpp remains unchanged; neither a negative PyTorch timing nor a short
screen authorizes native patching. Every release has its own reviewed, pushed
protocol, raw receipts, validation, issue and prerelease. No long matrix is planned.

The initial sequence is: (1) decision-sensitive single/pair interventions,
(2) structured plus independent spatial estimation, (3) recursive estimation with
missing observations, (4) information-directed acquisition versus contribution
ranking, (5) a physically charged policy or a distinct observation design if the
policy screen fails, (6) the strongest remaining hypothesis's bounded stress or
capacity test. Findings determine later protocols; these are not six frozen
performance claims. A failed prerequisite changes the next question, not its
published verdict. Reusing a measured development dataset will be declared.

## Inspirations and precise departures

- [Moraga, Bayesian spatial models](https://www.paulamoraga.com/book-spatial/bayesian-spatial-models.html):
  the BYM decomposition separates structured and independent variation. Our
  proposed transfer is a field of physical acquisition effects; layer adjacency
  is only a candidate geometry, not established spatial dependence.
- [Krause, Singh and Guestrin, 2008](https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume9/krause08a/krause08a.pdf):
  sensor placement can value observations for their predictive information about
  unobserved sites. We ask whether a paid weight-region probe can improve another
  loading decision. Their mutual-information guarantees do not automatically
  apply to token decisions, correlated acquisition costs or neural interactions.
- [Finley et al., modified predictive process](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2743161/):
  low-rank spatial approximation can require independent residual variance.
  A future low-rank controller must account for discarded uncertainty and memory;
  matching marginal variance does not recover all cross-site covariance.
- [Russell, rational metareasoning](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~russell/papers/aij-cnt.pdf):
  buying a computation has a decision benefit and a cost, including the controller.
  Lower prediction error or variance alone is not positive value of computation.
- [Chen et al., PMPD](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13461v2): phase-dependent precision
  motivates explicit prefill/decode rules. Its published gains are not ours.

The proposed novelty is an experimental combination and application, not invention
of Bayes filtering, quantization, sensor placement or speculative verification.
The first test also checks a prerequisite often hidden by additive utility scores:
two acquired regions can interact. We will measure their joint output directly.
