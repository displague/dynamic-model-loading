## Implementation, validation and cost

Pinned HF Qwen2.5 0.5B draft and 1.5B target, FP32/SDPA in the existing `.venv`.
Four-thread PyTorch and NumPy/OpenBLAS execution is enforced, with BLAS DLL hash.
Target-minus-draft centered logits train fit-only ridge heads from 16 projected
hidden features plus an intercept. All 151936 logits remain eligible; no KV or
backbone modification occurs. The entire 41,326,592 B FP32-equivalent head bank
would fit resident, so this is explicitly not a capacity or paging demonstration.

One checkpoint run: worker 18.932s + independent audit 4.485s. Second audit exactly
reproduces the complete summary in 4.329s without new inference. Readout and repeat
controls have zero relative-L2 error and identical argmax on the checked arrays.
No independent CPU replay of every neural forward is claimed.

Construction H2D: 8,150,989,056 B including registered buffers. Other explicit
copies: 40,896 B H2D and 190,160,896 B D2H. Sampled total GPU peak: 8,955,990,016 B;
sampled RSS peak: 9,446,006,784 B. No reduced-VRAM frontier or larger-model gain.
Artifact download/hash preparation took 14.155s separately; local rehashing and
model loading are inside the worker. Review fixed CLI failure propagation, buffer
accounting and BLAS threading before inference. **543 CPU tests** pass; final
clean-tip XML and commit-bound validation receipt accompany the release.
