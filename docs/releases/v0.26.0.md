# v0.26.0 - Physical output-margin sensors and selective-acquisition headroom

Second of six authorized bounded research releases. A **2 minute 25 second**
screen establishes useful page-level acquisition headroom and a measured
decision-sensitive observation. It remains a controlled, oracle-assisted research
result—not an online loader, speedup or larger-model capacity claim.

## Result

- Final FFN: 35 physical neuron pages, deliberately two-bit resident base,
  three refinement increments per page. Other FFNs use six-bit decode.
- Of 32 diagnostic positions, base differs from the high-eight draft at nine.
  A privileged **17-page oracle repairs 9/9**; fixed first-17 repairs 7/9.
  Both introduce two new draft-reference disagreements. **Haction passes.**
- Full diagnostic target agreement: base22/32, fixed29/32, oracle31/32,
  high-eight31/32. These are teacher-forced decisions, not accepted draft tokens.
- All **4,480** page-margin projections pass tolerance; maximum diagnostic
  absolute error **4.828e-6**. All 28 FFN numerical controls and eight full-model
  readout/KV replay checks pass. **Hobservation passes.**
- Mean paid page observation **3.532 ms**; full-model high-eight replay
  **374.273 ms**. Different work amounts: their ratio is **not a speedup claim**.
  Three diagnostic high-eight winners lie outside the base top-two; pairwise
  ordering does not certify full argmax.
- Research collection pays **65,379,336,192 H2D page bytes**, plus construction
  and other charged work. It observes every page before oracle selection and
  physically reloads both partial controls. No online traffic saving is claimed.
- Peak extra CUDA **907.302 MiB** under 1024 MiB. The final two-bit base saves
  10,321,920 resident bytes locally, but total sampled GPU peak is unchanged
  from v0.25. No larger-model capacity frontier was tested.

Worker **136.362 s** + independent CPU analysis **8.711 s**. A second model-free
audit reproduces the full summary exactly in **8.697 s**.
