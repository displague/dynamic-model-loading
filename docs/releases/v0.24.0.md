# Bayesian error prediction and explicit prefill-KV research

This prerelease implements and measures a per-layer Gaussian-process predictor
for unfetched FFN precision error, document-block calibration and high-precision
prefill with explicit speculative KV rules. It is novel-loading research code,
not a llama.cpp flag study or a claim of global methodological novelty.

## Results in 3 minutes 9 seconds

The supervised worker takes **179.970 seconds** and independent analysis
**8.985 seconds**. Two known diagnostic prompts, eight prefix/four output tokens
each, six conditions, one repetition. All 12 scored outputs and six warmups
match the scalar dense target. No long suite or native pivot follows.

There is a positive statistical component: GP mean log-error MAE is **0.06112**
versus **0.08806** for a constant per-layer mean, **30.59% lower**. But calibrated
upper coverage is **50/56 blocks (89.29%)**, below the frozen 90% criterion;
the constant bound covers 52/56. All observed shadow errors are below the fixed
acquisition threshold, so this subset does not exercise uncertainty's proposed
false-safe reduction. It is empirical coverage, not an exactness guarantee.

The GP mean requests no eighth-bit decode increments. Calibrated GP requests
five of 224, versus eight for the constant bound. It saves 18.75% H2D versus
all-eight but loses 12.5 percentage points of acceptance; its traffic advantage
over the constant controller is only 0.315%. Higher-precision prefill cannot
improve this subset's already-perfect six-bit acceptance. All four registered
compound gates fail; stop this candidate's expansion, not Bayesian research.
The resident scalar target takes 0.503 seconds; no acceleration over it is claimed.

## State, memory and interpretation

Draft prefill K/V remains byte-identical through precision changes and rejection.
Target and draft caches are independent FP32 tensors: eight-bit describes FFN
weights. Rejection crops both caches; accepted mixed-precision state remains;
fallback does not re-enter prefill. Prefix hashing/readback is charged to time.

Peak extra CUDA is **993.108 MiB** under the 1024 MiB allowance; sampled total GPU
peaks at **8987.098 MiB**. Original CPU weights, packed increments, staging,
workspace, projection, controller, target/draft residency and KV are charged.
Traffic savings do not imply VRAM savings. Shadow calibration/diagnostics consume
92.50 seconds and 114.53 GB of H2D; their setup cost is not hidden. No larger-model
capacity, long-session gain or reliable deployment speedup is established.

The result supports better prediction of local error magnitude, not yet better
acquisition. Selective higher precision is not monotonic in draft agreement in
this sample. A subsequent short protocol may test downstream/logit-sensitive
uncertainty; it has not been implemented or measured by this release.
