# v0.25.0 - Decision-field interventions, not an acquisition win

First of the owner's next six bounded research deliveries. The **1 minute 58
second** physical screen finds additive downstream effects but no useful repair
action among the four frozen regions. This is a research prerelease, not a
deployable loader, generated-acceptance result, speedup or larger-model claim.

## Measured result

- 384 full-model branches across 32 teacher-forced positions, with identical
  previously computed draft KV within each position. Four sites are promoted
  singly and in all six pairs, alongside base and same-history all-eight controls.
- **Hjoint passes:** additive logits predict all **48/48** diagnostic pair
  argmaxes; aggregate interaction norm is **6.836%** of joint-change norm.
- **Hdecision fails:** **0/8** diagnostic positions offer a singleton/pair repair.
  Base matches the target at 7/8; same-history all-eight at 8/8. All tested
  singleton/pair choices retain the base's wrong ID at the remaining position.
- All 28 numerical controls are exact against the frozen parent. Construction
  equality, actual page transfers, old-KV fingerprints and resource checks pass.
- **48,141,434,880 H2D bytes**, 4,664 cold loads, zero hits: fully charged research
  observations, not policy savings. Peak extra CUDA is **915.307 MiB** under the
  1024 MiB cap. Logical KV and cropped-view storage are recorded separately.
- Worker **110.121 s**, initial independent analysis **8.119 s**. A second,
  model-free audit reproduces the full summary exactly in **22.475 s**.

## Decision and next step

Stop expansion of this four-region action set. A successful additive fit is not
information value when none of the affordable tested actions repairs the error.
Only two reused diagnostic documents were tested; there is no significance or
generalization claim. The next short protocol will change the acquisition unit
and observation cost near the output decision, not lower Hdecision's bar.
