memory accounting, source freeze, all 411 archived members, and the negative-result
and novelty/32B/native boundaries. Its one minor finding was a stale historical
roadmap sentence naming ADR 0003 as current. That sentence now explicitly identifies
ADR 0003 as historical and ADR 0004/0005 as current.

reproduced the summary and all-file inventory byte-for-byte; the corresponding
